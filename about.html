THE IDEA BEHIND EDUMARTH

One place for your everyday essentials.

Student days can move quickly: a morning lecture, an assignment deadline, a commute, then another study session. EduMarth is designed around those everyday moments.

Our catalogue brings together four useful areas: study essentials, assignment tools, everyday campus items, and learning and revision resources. Instead of a crowded page, we focus on a collection you can search, filter and compare with confidence.

EduMarth is a Group 3 capstone project. It is a learning environment where we put interface design, usability and software development into practice.

WHAT GUIDES US

Simple choices. A clearer day.

01 / OUR MISSION

Make essentials easier to find.

Help students explore useful products in a clear catalogue, with readable details, straightforward categories and practical search tools.

02 / OUR VISION

A useful companion to student life.

Build towards a student-focused experience that helps people find tools for their own routines, from coursework to everyday campus life.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Design with clarity and honesty.

Keep the experience understandable. Show what a feature does, explain the limits of our prototype and use feedback to guide improvements.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

About this version.

Can I buy products from EduMarth?

Not in this version. EduMarth is a student demonstration. The cart helps demonstrate browsing and selection, but no order is created and no payment is collected.

Are the products and prices real?

The catalogue uses sample products, stock and AUD prices for our capstone. Product illustrations are concepts. Comparison prices and demo deals are fictional.

What happens to the items in my cart?

They are saved in your current browser when browser storage is available. Your cart does not sync to other devices, and clearing browser data may remove it.

How can I reach the project team?

Use the contact form to save an enquiry in the project database. You will receive an enquiry reference on screen. Email notifications are not enabled.
