Before you get in touch.
Will this form send an email?
No. Submitting saves your enquiry to the project database and gives you a reference number. Email delivery is not configured.
Can you help with an order or payment?
This is a student demonstration and does not process orders or payments. Please use the form for catalogue questions, feedback or project enquiries.
What should I include in a bug report?
Include the page, device/browser, steps you took and what went wrong. Avoid adding passwords, payment information or other sensitive details.
