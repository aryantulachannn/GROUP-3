OUR PURPOSE

Student life has enough moving parts.

Finding your everyday essentials should feel simple. EduMarth brings study tools, assignment tech, learning resources and campus favourites into one clear, organised collection.

Whether you’re setting up a study corner, planning your semester or packing for a busy day, our aim is to make it easier to find what fits your routine and budget.

Get to know EduMarth ↗

A student project with a clear purpose.

Created by Group 3 for our capstone, EduMarth explores how thoughtful design can make browsing and comparing student essentials more useful.

  • Find your focus. Tools for studying, planning and getting organised.
  • Choose with clarity. Clear categories, prices and product details.
  • Help us improve. Your feedback shapes our next steps.
